Understanding Blood Types

Learn about different blood types, their characteristics, and compatibility for donation and transfusion.

A

Type A

Has A antigens on red blood cells and B antibodies in plasma

Can donate to: A, AB

Can receive from: A, O

B

Type B

Has B antigens on red blood cells and A antibodies in plasma

Can donate to: B, AB

Can receive from: B, O

AB

Type AB

Has both A and B antigens on red blood cells and no antibodies in plasma

Can donate to: AB only

Can receive from: All types (Universal recipient)

O

Type O

Has no antigens on red blood cells and both A and B antibodies in plasma

Can donate to: All types (Universal donor)

Can receive from: O only

About Us

Our Mission

The Blood Bank Management System is dedicated to connecting blood donors with recipients in need, ensuring a reliable and efficient blood supply chain that saves lives every day.

Our Story

Founded in 2020, our platform was created by a team of healthcare professionals and technology experts who recognized the need for a more streamlined approach to blood donation and distribution.

Our Impact

Since our inception, we have:

  • Connected over 1,000 donors with recipients in need
  • Facilitated more than 500 successful blood transfusions
  • Partnered with 25 hospitals and healthcare facilities
  • Expanded our services to 10 cities nationwide

Our Team

Our dedicated team consists of medical professionals, software developers, and customer support specialists who work tirelessly to ensure the platform operates smoothly and efficiently.
